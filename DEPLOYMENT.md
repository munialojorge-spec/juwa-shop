## **CUSTOM DOMAIN SETUP** 🌐

After deploying to Netlify:

1. **Buy a domain** from:
   - GoDaddy ($10/year)
   - Namecheap ($8/year)
   - Google Domains ($12/year)

2. **In Netlify Dashboard:**
   - Click your site
   - Go to "Domain management"
   - Click "Add custom domain"
   - Enter your domain
   - Follow DNS setup instructions
